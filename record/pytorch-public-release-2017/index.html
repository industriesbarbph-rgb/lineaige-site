A first-party PyTorch retrospective published January 19, 2018 states that the date marked one year since PyTorch was released publicly, supporting January 19, 2017 as the public-release date. A first-party Facebook AI Research retrospective independently states that PyTorch was announced at the beginning of 2017 and describes it as an open-source project. This candidate records the public-release milestone only; it does not infer PyTorch's invention date, development geography, universal priority, ancestry, influence, causality, or relationship edges.
