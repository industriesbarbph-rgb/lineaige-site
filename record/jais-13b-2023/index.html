Jais 13B Open Release

Inception, Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), and Cerebras Systems announced the open release of Jais on August 30, 2023. The released Jais model had 13 billion parameters and was trained on a purpose-built Arabic-English dataset; the collaborating team also released an instruction-tuned Jais-chat variant. First-party institutional announcements also state that Jais was trained on the Condor Galaxy 1 supercomputer.

MBZUAI's first-party August 30, 2023 announcement states that Inception announced the open-source release of Jais, identifies the collaboration with MBZUAI and Cerebras, describes the released model as a 13-billion-parameter Arabic-centric LLM, and states that it was trained on Condor Galaxy 1. The authors' Jais/Jais-chat paper was submitted to arXiv the same day and independently documents the release of the Jais foundation model and Jais-chat instruction-tuned variant. G42's same-day institutional announcement independently corroborates the open-source release, collaboration, model scale, and Condor Galaxy 1 training system.
