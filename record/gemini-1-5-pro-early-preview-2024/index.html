Gemini 1.5 Pro Early Preview

Google announced Gemini 1.5 on February 15, 2024 and began a limited early preview of Gemini 1.5 Pro for developers and enterprise customers through AI Studio and Vertex AI.

Google's first-party February 15, 2024 announcement states that Gemini 1.5 was announced that day, that Gemini 1.5 Pro was the first 1.5 model released for early testing, and that starting that day a limited group of developers and enterprise customers could try it via AI Studio and Vertex AI in private/limited preview. The same source explicitly says a wider release would come later, so this record is classified as an early-preview event rather than general availability.
