Gemma-APS Release

Google released Gemma-APS in 2B and 7B sizes on October 15, 2024. Google's official documentation describes Gemma-APS as a Gemma variant for abstractive proposition segmentation that segments passages into individual facts, statements, and ideas and restates them as full sentences with small changes to the source text.
