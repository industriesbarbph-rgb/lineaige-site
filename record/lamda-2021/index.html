LaMDA Research Introduction

Google introduced LaMDA, short for Language Model for Dialogue Applications, in an official Google I/O research announcement on May 18, 2021.

Google's first-party May 18, 2021 announcement explicitly introduces LaMDA as a language model for dialogue applications and describes it as open-domain and built on Transformer. The later LaMDA paper, submitted to arXiv on January 20, 2022, independently corroborates the LaMDA identity and describes a family of Transformer-based neural language models specialized for dialog, with models up to 137B parameters. This record intentionally uses the dated 2021 first-party research introduction as its event boundary. It does not treat that date as a public product launch, API release, unrestricted model-weight release, deployment date, or evidence of causal influence on later systems. No geography, ancestry, influence, causality, or relationships are inferred.
