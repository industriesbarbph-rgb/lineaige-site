Meta AI's first-party May 3, 2022 announcement states that it was sharing OPT-175B, a 175-billion-parameter language model trained on publicly available datasets, for research access. The associated paper's arXiv record was submitted May 2, 2022 and describes OPT as a suite of decoder-only pretrained transformers from 125M to 175B parameters, with code and a training logbook released and model access intended for interested researchers. This record is intentionally classified as a research-access announcement, not a general-public product launch, unrestricted open-weight release, API launch, deployment date, or causal ancestor of later models.
