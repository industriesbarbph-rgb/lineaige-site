IBM Watson wins the Jeopardy! challenge

IBM's Watson question-answering system defeated Jeopardy! champions Ken Jennings and Brad Rutter in a televised contest conducted over February 14–16, 2011. IBM's retrospective records Watson's final winning score as USD 77,147, ahead of Jennings and Rutter.
