T5 Research Publication

Google researchers submitted the T5 paper, 'Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer,' on October 23, 2019, introducing a unified text-to-text framework for transfer learning in NLP.

Why it matters

A documented language-model research milestone that framed diverse text-based NLP tasks within a single text-to-text formulation and released supporting data, pretrained models, and code for reproducibility.

The primary paper was submitted to arXiv on October 23, 2019 and explicitly introduces a unified framework that converts text-based language problems into a text-to-text format, together with the Colossal Clean Crawled Corpus and released pretrained models/code. Google Research later described the resulting model as the Text-To-Text Transfer Transformer (T5) and reported an 11-billion-parameter largest model.
