Shakey the Robot

SRI began the Shakey research program in 1966. Shakey combined mobile robotics with perception, reasoning, planning and route-finding in a single research system developed at SRI's Artificial Intelligence Center.

SRI's first-party institutional history states that Shakey was the subject of its Artificial Intelligence Center research from 1966 to 1972 and describes its perception, reasoning, planning, route-finding and object-rearrangement capabilities. The record is intentionally stored at year precision because the reviewed evidence supports the research start year, not a separately verified exact system-birth day.
