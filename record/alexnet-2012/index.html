ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks

Alex Krizhevsky, Ilya Sutskever and Geoffrey E. Hinton published ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks in the 2012 Neural Information Processing Systems proceedings, reporting a large deep convolutional neural network trained for ImageNet classification and substantially lower test error than the comparison results described in the paper.
