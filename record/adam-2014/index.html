Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization

Diederik P. Kingma and Jimmy Ba submitted 'Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization' on December 22, 2014. The paper introduces Adam, a first-order gradient-based optimization algorithm for stochastic objective functions based on adaptive estimates of lower-order moments.
