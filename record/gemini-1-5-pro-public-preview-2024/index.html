Gemini 1.5 Pro Public Preview

Google announced on May 14, 2024 that Gemini 1.5 Pro was available in public preview in Google AI Studio and Vertex AI, alongside Gemini 1.5 Flash, across more than 200 countries and territories.

Google's first-party May 14, 2024 developer announcement states that Gemini 1.5 Pro and Gemini 1.5 Flash were available that day in more than 200 countries and territories in preview and would be generally available in June. A separate first-party Google Gemini-family announcement explicitly calls both models available in public preview in Google AI Studio and Vertex AI. This record therefore uses May 14 only for the public-preview boundary; it does not treat that date as general availability. Geography here describes service availability stated by Google, not development origin. No ancestry, influence, causality, universal priority, or relationship edges are inferred.
