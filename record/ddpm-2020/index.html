Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models

Jonathan Ho, Ajay Jain, and Pieter Abbeel submitted 'Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models' to arXiv on June 19, 2020. The paper reports high-quality image synthesis using diffusion probabilistic models, with training based on a weighted variational bound connected to denoising score matching with Langevin dynamics.
