U-Net Research Publication

Olaf Ronneberger, Philipp Fischer, and Thomas Brox submitted 'U-Net: Convolutional Networks for Biomedical Image Segmentation' on May 18, 2015. The paper presented a network and training strategy for biomedical image segmentation built around a contracting path for context and a symmetric expanding path for precise localization.
