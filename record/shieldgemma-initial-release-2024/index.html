ShieldGemma Initial Release

Google's official Gemma release ledger records July 31, 2024 as the initial release of ShieldGemma. Google's official ShieldGemma model card describes the initial family as safety content moderation models built on Gemma 2, available in 2B, 9B, and 27B parameter sizes.
