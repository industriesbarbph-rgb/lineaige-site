Gemini 2.0 Flash Experimental Release

Google announced Gemini 2.0 on December 11, 2024 and released an experimental version of Gemini 2.0 Flash, the first model in the Gemini 2.0 family. Google also made a chat-optimized version of Gemini 2.0 Flash Experimental available to Gemini users that day.
