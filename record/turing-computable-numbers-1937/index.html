On Computable Numbers, with an Application to the Entscheidungsproblem

Alan Turing's paper On Computable Numbers, with an Application to the Entscheidungsproblem was received on May 28, 1936 and is recorded by Oxford Academic as published on January 1, 1937. The paper formalized computability using abstract machines and applied the framework to the Entscheidungsproblem.
