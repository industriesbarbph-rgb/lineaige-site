Some Studies in Machine Learning Using the Game of Checkers

Arthur L. Samuel published 'Some Studies in Machine Learning Using the Game of Checkers' in the IBM Journal of Research and Development on July 31, 1959. The paper reports two machine-learning procedures investigated through a checkers program and describes experiments in which the program improved its play through learning procedures.
