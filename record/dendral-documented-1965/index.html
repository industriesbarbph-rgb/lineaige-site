DENDRAL Documented in Stanford Machine-Induction Research Memo

On April 5, 1965, Edward A. Feigenbaum and R. W. Watson issued An Initial Problem Statement for a Machine Induction Research Project at the Stanford Artificial Intelligence Project. The memo described research then getting under way on machine induction using organic chemistry and explicitly documented a system called Dendral as an existing ALGOL program for the B-5000.
