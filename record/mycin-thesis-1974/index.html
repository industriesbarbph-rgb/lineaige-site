MYCIN thesis milestone

Edward H. Shortliffe's Stanford thesis documented MYCIN as a rule-based computer program designed to advise physicians regarding antimicrobial therapy selection. Stanford's computer-science report bibliography lists the thesis in October 1974 and describes MYCIN's consultation, explanation, and rule-acquisition components.
