An Image is Worth 16x16 Words: Transformers for Image Recognition at Scale

Alexey Dosovitskiy and coauthors submitted 'An Image is Worth 16x16 Words: Transformers for Image Recognition at Scale' on October 22, 2020. The paper describes applying a pure Transformer directly to sequences of image patches and names the resulting model Vision Transformer (ViT).
