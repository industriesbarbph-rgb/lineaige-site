GoogLeNet / Inception Research Publication

Christian Szegedy and coauthors submitted 'Going Deeper with Convolutions' on September 17, 2014, presenting the Inception deep convolutional architecture and identifying GoogLeNet as a 22-layer incarnation used for ILSVRC 2014.
