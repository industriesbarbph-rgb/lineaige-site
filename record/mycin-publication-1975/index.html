MYCIN — 1975 Clinical Therapeutics Publication

In August 1975, Edward H. Shortliffe, Randall Davis, Stanton G. Axline, Bruce G. Buchanan, C. Cordell Green, and Stanley N. Cohen published a paper describing progress on MYCIN, an interactive computer program designed to advise physicians about selecting antimicrobial therapy for hospital patients with bacterial infections.
