Self-organized formation of topologically correct feature maps

Teuvo Kohonen published 'Self-organized formation of topologically correct feature maps' in Biological Cybernetics in January 1982. The paper studied and simulated a self-organizing process in which signal representations become arranged into topologically ordered output responses.
