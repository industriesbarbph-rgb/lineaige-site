LeNet-5 — 1998 Proceedings of the IEEE Publication Milestone

In November 1998, Yann LeCun, Léon Bottou, Yoshua Bengio, and Patrick Haffner published "Gradient-Based Learning Applied to Document Recognition" in Proceedings of the IEEE. The paper describes gradient-based learning for document recognition and presents the LeNet-5 convolutional neural-network architecture in that publication context.
