Neural networks and physical systems with emergent collective computational abilities

John J. Hopfield published Neural networks and physical systems with emergent collective computational abilities in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences in April 1982. The paper described a model in which collective properties of many simple neuron-like components produce content-addressable memory through asynchronous parallel processing.
