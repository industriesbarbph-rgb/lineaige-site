Highly accurate protein structure prediction with AlphaFold

John Jumper and colleagues published Highly accurate protein structure prediction with AlphaFold in Nature on July 15, 2021. The paper describes the redesigned AlphaFold neural-network system and reports CASP14 performance with accuracy competitive with experimental structures in a majority of cases.
