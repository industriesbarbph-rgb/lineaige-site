Some methods for classification and analysis of multivariate observations

J. MacQueen published 'Some methods for classification and analysis of multivariate observations' in the Proceedings of the Fifth Berkeley Symposium on Mathematical Statistics and Probability in 1967. The paper describes a process for partitioning an N-dimensional population into k sets and explicitly calls the process 'k-means.'
