A Logical Calculus of the Ideas Immanent in Nervous Activity

Warren S. McCulloch and Walter Pitts published A Logical Calculus of the Ideas Immanent in Nervous Activity in The Bulletin of Mathematical Biophysics in December 1943, describing neural activity in terms of propositional logic and formal networks.
