Sequence to Sequence Learning with Neural Networks

Ilya Sutskever, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc V. Le submitted 'Sequence to Sequence Learning with Neural Networks' on September 10, 2014, presenting an end-to-end sequence-learning approach that uses one multilayer LSTM to encode an input sequence into a fixed-dimensional vector and another deep LSTM to decode the target sequence.
