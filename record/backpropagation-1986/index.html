Learning representations by back-propagating errors

David E. Rumelhart, Geoffrey E. Hinton and Ronald J. Williams published Learning representations by back-propagating errors in Nature on 9 October 1986, describing a learning procedure that repeatedly adjusts connection weights in networks of neuron-like units to reduce the difference between actual and desired outputs.
