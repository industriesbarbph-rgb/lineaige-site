A Learning Algorithm for Boltzmann Machines

David H. Ackley, Geoffrey E. Hinton, and Terrence J. Sejnowski published "A Learning Algorithm for Boltzmann Machines" in Cognitive Science in January 1985. The paper describes a general parallel search method based on statistical mechanics and a learning rule for modifying connection strengths to incorporate task-domain knowledge.
