ERNIE: Enhanced Representation through Knowledge Integration

Yu Sun, Shuohuan Wang, Yukun Li, Shikun Feng, Xuyi Chen, Han Zhang, Xin Tian, Danxiang Zhu, Hao Tian, and Hua Wu submitted the paper 'ERNIE: Enhanced Representation through Knowledge Integration' on April 19, 2019. The paper described ERNIE as a language representation model using knowledge-oriented masking strategies, including entity-level and phrase-level masking.
