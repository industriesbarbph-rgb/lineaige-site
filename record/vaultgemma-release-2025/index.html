VaultGemma Release

Google's Gemma release ledger records September 13, 2025 as the release of VaultGemma in 1B parameter size. A Google Research post published September 12, 2025 introduced VaultGemma and stated that model weights were being released on Hugging Face and Kaggle alongside a technical report.
