Aya 23 Public Launch

Cohere For AI announced Aya 23 on May 23, 2024 as a family of multilingual generative language models covering 23 languages, with 8B and 35B parameter open-weight models. The accompanying primary technical report documents the Aya 23 family and release of both models' open weights.
