Masakhane -- Machine Translation For Africa

Iroro Orife and collaborators submitted 'Masakhane -- Machine Translation For Africa' on March 13, 2020. The paper documents Masakhane as an open-source, continent-wide, distributed, online research effort focused on machine translation for African languages and describes its community-building and research methodology.

The original arXiv record establishes the March 13 2020 submission date, author list, and characterization of Masakhane as an open-source, continent-wide, distributed, online research effort for machine translation for African languages. ACL Anthology's later 2020 Findings paper independently documents Masakhane's participatory machine-translation work, benchmarks, datasets, models, code, and evaluation results across African languages. 'Continent-wide' is retained only as the project's own scope characterization and is not converted into a development-location claim. No specific country of origin, institutional affiliation, ancestry, influence, causality, or relationship is inferred.
