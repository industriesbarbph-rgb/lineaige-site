Flamingo Research Introduction

Google DeepMind's first-party research post is dated April 28, 2022 and explicitly says it introduces Flamingo as a single visual language model for few-shot learning over open-ended multimodal tasks. The associated arXiv preprint was submitted April 29, 2022 and describes Flamingo as a family of visual language models that can ingest interleaved visual and textual data. This record intentionally uses the dated first-party research introduction as the event boundary; it does not treat that date as a product launch, API release, unrestricted public-weight release, deployment date, or evidence of causal influence on later systems. No geography, ancestry, influence, causality, or relationships are inferred.
