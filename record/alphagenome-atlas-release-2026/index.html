AlphaGenome Atlas Release

Google DeepMind introduced AlphaGenome Atlas on September 8, 2026, describing it as a platform containing molecular-effect predictions for 9 billion single-nucleotide variants in the human genome and making it available for academic research through a free website portal.

Google DeepMind's first-party announcement is dated September 8, 2026 and explicitly introduces AlphaGenome Atlas, states that it contains predictions for 9 billion single-nucleotide variants, and says it is available for academic research through a free website portal. Google DeepMind's AlphaGenome science page independently describes the Atlas as a 1PB dataset containing every possible nucleotide variant with an AlphaGenome Variant Impact score and says it is available to researchers around the world.
