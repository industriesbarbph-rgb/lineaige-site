WABOT-1 — 1973 Waseda Completion Milestone

Waseda University records that Professor Ichiro Kato's team completed WABOT-1 in 1973. University histories describe the humanoid robot as capable of bipedal walking and simple conversation; later Waseda material also describes object grasping and simple Japanese communication.
