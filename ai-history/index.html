Selected AI history milestones

Summer 1956

Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence

In the summer of 1956, a small group of scientists gathered at Dartmouth College for the Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence.

1958

The Perceptron

Frank Rosenblatt published The Perceptron: A Probabilistic Model for Information Storage and Organization in the Brain in Psychological Review.

April 1960

Recursive Functions of Symbolic Expressions and Their Computation by Machine, Part I

John McCarthy published 'Recursive Functions of Symbolic Expressions and Their Computation by Machine, Part I' in April 1960.

April 5, 1965

DENDRAL Documented in Stanford Machine-Induction Research Memo

On April 5, 1965, Edward A. Feigenbaum and R. W. Watson issued An Initial Problem Statement for a Machine Induction Research Project at the Stanford Artificial Intelligence Project.

1970

SHRDLU development period completed

Terry Winograd later documented that he developed SHRDLU at MIT between 1968 and 1970.

9 October 1986

Learning representations by back-propagating errors

David E. Rumelhart, Geoffrey E. Hinton and Ronald J. Williams published Learning representations by back-propagating errors in Nature on 9 October 1986.

11 May 1997

Deep Blue defeats Garry Kasparov

IBM's Deep Blue defeated reigning world chess champion Garry Kasparov in a six-game match completed on 11 May 1997, winning 3.5 to 2.5 under standard tournament time controls.

2012

ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks

Alex Krizhevsky, Ilya Sutskever and Geoffrey E. Hinton published ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks in the 2012 Neural Information Processing Systems.

2017

Attention Is All You Need

Vaswani and colleagues introduced the Transformer architecture in Attention Is All You Need.

30 November 2022

Introducing ChatGPT

OpenAI publicly introduced ChatGPT on November 30, 2022 as a conversational model released for research preview and user feedback.

March 14, 2023

GPT-4 Public Introduction

OpenAI publicly introduced GPT-4 on March 14, 2023. OpenAI described GPT-4 as a large multimodal model capable of accepting image and text inputs and producing text outputs.

15 February 2024

Gemini 1.5 Pro Early Preview

Google announced Gemini 1.5 on February 15, 2024 and began a limited early preview of Gemini 1.5 Pro for developers and enterprise customers through AI Studio and Vertex AI.

January 20, 2025

DeepSeek-R1 Release

DeepSeek publicly released DeepSeek-R1 on January 20, 2025.

Before 1956: foundations and precursors

These verified records predate the main AI-history timeline and are kept in their own layer.

Received 28 May 1936 · Published 1 January 1937 · PRECURSOR

On Computable Numbers, with an Application to the Entscheidungsproblem

Alan Turing's paper On Computable Numbers, with an Application to the Entscheidungsproblem was received on May 28, 1936 and is recorded by Oxford Academic as published on January 1, 1937.

December 1943 · PRECURSOR

A Logical Calculus of the Ideas Immanent in Nervous Activity

Warren S. McCulloch and Walter Pitts published A Logical Calculus of the Ideas Immanent in Nervous Activity in The Bulletin of Mathematical Biophysics in December 1943.

March 1950 · PRECURSOR

Programming a Computer for Playing Chess

Claude E. Shannon published Programming a Computer for Playing Chess in Philosophical Magazine in March 1950.

1950 · PRECURSOR

Computing Machinery and Intelligence

Alan Turing published Computing Machinery and Intelligence in Mind, framing machine intelligence through the imitation game and asking whether machines can think.

1955 · PRECURSOR

Dartmouth Artificial Intelligence Proposal

John McCarthy, Marvin Minsky, Nathaniel Rochester and Claude Shannon authored the proposal for the Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence, dated August 31, 1955.
