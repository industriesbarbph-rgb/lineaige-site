LINEAiGE Evidence Methodology

LINEAiGE is an evidence-controlled historical data system. The visual timeline is one way of viewing the record; it is not the record itself.

Historical boundary: 1956

LINEAiGE treats 1956 as the beginning of artificial intelligence as a recognized research field, following the Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence. Verified records before 1956 are preserved in a separate prehistory / foundations / precursors layer. They remain sourced, searchable, and crawlable, but they are not counted as AI-field history and do not extend the main AI ruler earlier than 1956.

Primary-source preference

LINEAiGE prefers first-party and primary evidence whenever available.

Chronology is not causality

Events can be ordered in time without claiming that one caused another. Causal relationships require their own evidence.

Date precision

LINEAiGE does not manufacture precision. If evidence supports only a year or month, the record preserves that level.

Separate evidence layers

Prehistory/precursors, AI history from 1956 onward, learning resources, contextual media, candidate research, and announced future milestones remain semantically separate.

Announced future

A future record remains an announcement until evidence confirms, updates, delays, cancels, or completes it.

Empty years and research gaps

An empty year means only that LINEAiGE has no admitted canonical milestone assigned to that year. It is not automatically a research gap. A year is described as a research gap only when the research process explicitly identifies unresolved historical coverage.

LINEAiGE preserves chronology, evidence status, and source provenance. An announcement is not completed history until confirmed.
